## 2. What may we put into Claude?

### The answer so far
The pilot may use its own planning notes, decisions that have been approved, process notes, and made-up figures for practice.

The pilot may not use anything about a named employee's case or record, passwords or access details, or material that belongs to the organisations we serve.

If you are not sure whether something is allowed, ask before you paste it in. Questions go to the information governance adviser. Sharing or publishing anything is a separate decision for a person, not something Claude decides.

Source: What the pilot may use, 5 February 2027, Information governance. Check first, because the note has no named owner.

### Sources that could answer it
- What the pilot may use, 5 February 2027: Check first

- Organisation overview, 2 February 2027: Trusted. It says the same about Ruth Evans.
- HR case notes, 5 February 2027: Excluded
