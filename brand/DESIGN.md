## Lettering
- font.display: Bebas Neue, for headings. Current. Leila's guide, 5 February 2027.
- font.body: Aptos, for body text. Current. Leila's guide, 5 February 2027.
- Fallbacks: where Aptos is missing, use the system sans-serif. Where Bebas Neue is missing, use a condensed sans-serif in capitals. Provisional.
- Body size: 18px, line spacing 1.5. Provisional. Maya Patel, 1 March 2027: the guide gives the typeface but not a size.
- Heading sizes: 40px for the page heading, 28px for section headings, 22px for small headings, all in Bebas Neue. Provisional.

## Rules in use
1. Use one highlight colour for one job. If violet marks "something you can act on", it marks that and nothing else. Current. Leila's guide, 5 February 2027.
2. Highlights are for filled shapes only, never for small text. Current. Leila's guide, 5 February 2027.
3. Headings in Bebas Neue, body in Aptos. Current. Leila's guide, 5 February 2027.
4. Light ground, charcoal text. Current. Leila's guide, 5 February 2027.
5. Which job each highlight does: violet for something you can act on, green for a key figure, pink for a note to read. Provisional. Violet's job comes from the example in the guide. The other two jobs are a guess.
6. Panels use the off-white ground or one of the three highlight fills. No other colour. Current. Maya Patel's decision, 1 March 2027. It follows from the colours in Leila's guide, 5 February 2027.
