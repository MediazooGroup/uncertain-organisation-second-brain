# Source: What the pilot may use

- Date: 5 February 2027
- Owner: Ruth Evans, information governance adviser. Confirmed by Maya Patel, 15 February 2027, after checking with Ruth. The note itself is signed only "Information governance".
- Label: Trusted (changed from Check first on 15 February 2027, when the owner was confirmed)
- What it is: The rules on what material the pilot may and may not put into Claude.

## Useful text
- May use: the pilot's own planning notes, decisions that have been approved, process notes, and made-up figures for practice.
- May not use: anything about a named employee's case or record, passwords or access details, or material that belongs to the organisations we serve.
- Sharing or publishing anything is a separate decision for a person, not something Claude decides.
- If you are not sure whether something is allowed, ask before you paste it in. Questions go to the information governance adviser.
