## The Uncertain Organisation pilot team
One home for what the twelve pilot members know about their budget, their rules and who checks what. Every line says where it came from. The pilot is a small learning pilot, not a roll-out.

### The questions this second brain answers

1. **Which budget may the pilot spend?** £8,000, approved by Daniel Reed on 5 February 2027 for this pilot only. Look in [What was decided](knowledge/decisions.md).
2. **What may we put into Claude?** The pilot's own planning notes, approved decisions, process notes and made-up practice figures. Nothing about a named employee's case or record, no passwords or access details, nothing that belongs to the organisations we serve. If you are not sure, ask before you paste it in. Look in [What we know](knowledge/facts.md), facts F4 to F8.
3. **Who checks what the pilot makes?** Daniel Reed checks facts and approves spending (Provisional: it rests on Maya Patel's notes). Who checks anything else: Unknown. Look in [What we know](knowledge/facts.md) F9 and [Still to find out](knowledge/questions.md) Q3.
4. **Is the pilot ready to roll out?** Unknown. Look in [Still to find out](knowledge/questions.md) Q5 and Q6.

### Where to look
- [What we know](knowledge/facts.md): facts, each with its label and where it came from.
- [What was decided](knowledge/decisions.md): decisions by someone with the right to choose.
- [Still to find out](knowledge/questions.md): open questions, each Unknown.
- [Who is who](knowledge/people-and-roles.md): names and roles.

Labels: **Current** is true now, with a source. **Provisional** is not settled yet. **Unknown** means nobody has said. **Replaced** is an older version, kept with its date.
