# The Uncertain Organisation pilot: project note

## Team
The pilot team at The Uncertain Organisation, a 180-person UK service organisation. Twelve people are trying a practical way of working with Claude. Coordinated by Maya Patel, L&D lead.

## The need
People keep asking what the pilot may spend and what they may put into Claude, and nobody has one place to look.

## Who it is for
The twelve people in the pilot, and Daniel Reed and Ruth Evans, who get asked the same things over and over.

## What we may use
Our own planning notes, decisions that have been approved, and process notes.

## What stays out
Anything about a named employee.

Ruth Evans, our information governance adviser, is writing the full rules this week. The rest is to follow.

## Who decides
- What may go into Claude: Ruth Evans, information governance adviser.
- What the pilot may spend: Daniel Reed, operations manager.

## What success looks like
Someone on the pilot can find the answer to either of those questions and see where it came from.

Started: 1 February 2027, Week 0 lab.
