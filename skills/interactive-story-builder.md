# Interactive story builder: my changes

These are the changes I chose to how this skill works for me. Newest last. A change can alter wording, order and emphasis, never the truth rules.

## 2027-04-26 (Week 12 lab, Maya Patel)
- Instruction: Every number on the page opens its own source, date, out of how many and over what period. That includes counts of people, minutes and money, wherever they appear. A number that cannot open a source is left out, and the learner is told.
- Why: the first version of the page had numbers with no tap, such as "Twelve people" in the opening, "three of the twelve" and the 75 and 50 minutes in the open list, and the £8,000 under unspent money. The old wording left the choice of proof to me.
- Tricky example: a number with no source, "The pilot cut update time by a third across the team", and a number with a source but no tap, "Three of the twelve did not come".
- Test showed: the line with no source was left out both times, so it showed nothing new. "Three of the twelve" stood with no tap under the old instruction and opened the attendance note of 8 February 2027 under the new one. I kept the change.
